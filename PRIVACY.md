# SafeSpeed UK privacy policy

**ProtoSoft** makes the SafeSpeed UK app for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, what leaves your phone, and who receives it.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Contact: protosoftuk+privacy@gmail.com

## The short version

- SafeSpeed UK has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics. ProtoSoft does not track you.
- Your zones, settings and drive history stay on your phone.
- To show the speed limit of the road you're on, the app sends your location to public OpenStreetMap servers. You can turn this off.
- ProtoSoft only receives information from you if you choose to email us a crash report.

## Your location

SafeSpeed UK needs your phone's precise location to work. It uses it to measure your speed, time you between average speed cameras and warn you about fixed cameras. That measuring is done on your phone.

Your location leaves your phone in these cases:

| When | What is sent | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Road speed limits** is on (Settings › Road speed limits; on by default) | Your position, about once every kilometre while you're tracking a drive | Public OpenStreetMap lookup servers ("Overpass"): overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee or overpass.kumi.systems |
| You mark a camera while driving, or place a camera or zone on the map | The position of that camera or point | The same lookup servers |
| You open the Map | Which area of the map you're looking at, which is around you when the map is following you | The map server, tiles.openfreemap.org |
| You download zones (Get UK zones, or Get OSM zones here) | The area you asked for | The same lookup servers |
| You add, move or download a zone | The positions of that zone's two cameras, so the road between them can be worked out | Public OpenStreetMap routing servers: routing.openstreetmap.de or router.project-osrm.org |

These requests are sent over an encrypted connection. They don't contain your name, an account or any identifier made by the app. Like any internet request, they do reach the server from your phone's internet (IP) address.

These servers are run by organisations and volunteers in the OpenStreetMap community, not by ProtoSoft. ProtoSoft never receives your location and has no copy of it. We can't say what each server keeps in its own logs; that is covered by each operator's own policy. The servers may be outside the UK.

**How to turn it off:** switch off **Settings › Road speed limits** to stop the regular position lookups. Zone timing and camera alerts carry on working, because they use the zones and cameras already stored on your phone.

When your phone is connected to Android Auto, the app can also read the car's own GPS position and wheel speed. These are used on your phone in the same way as the phone's GPS and are not sent anywhere else.

## What stays on your phone

- Your zones and cameras, including ones you add or edit
- Your settings
- Your drive history (the zones you drove through, when, and your average speed)
- Limits you set for today
- The crash log (see below)

ProtoSoft can't see any of this.

**Backup files.** Zones › ⋮ › Back up saves your zones, settings and drive history to a file in a place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

**Android's own backup.** If you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include SafeSpeed UK's data in your phone's backup, in your own Google account. That is controlled in your phone's settings and handled by Google, not by ProtoSoft.

## Crash reports

If SafeSpeed UK stops unexpectedly, it saves a report on your phone (Settings › Crash log). A report contains the time, the app version, your phone's make and model, its Android version, and the technical details of the error. It doesn't contain your location, zones or drive history.

Reports stay on your phone and the app never sends them itself. If you choose **Send to developer**, your own email app opens with the report in a message addressed to ProtoSoft, and it is only sent if you press Send. If you send it, we receive the report, your email address and anything you add to the message. We use these only to find and fix the problem and to reply to you if needed. We delete them once the problem is dealt with, and within 12 months at most.

You can delete the saved reports at any time with **Delete all** on the Crash log screen.

## Buying the app

SafeSpeed UK is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. ProtoSoft never sees your card details. Google shows us a record of each order (such as an order number, the date and the price), which we keep as long as accounting and tax rules require.

## What we don't do

- No adverts
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No accounts or sign-in
- No selling or renting of personal information
- No collecting of contacts, photos, messages or anything else from your phone

## Other services the app relies on

- **Google Play services** provides your phone's location to the app. This is part of Android on your phone and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
- **Android Auto** (if you use it) shows SafeSpeed UK on your car's screen. It is a Google service covered by Google's privacy policy.
- **Links** in the app (for example to OpenStreetMap's licence) open in your web browser, where that website's own policy applies.

## Children

SafeSpeed UK is for drivers. It is not aimed at children.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. In practice the only personal information ProtoSoft holds is any crash report email you have sent us and the order records Google Play gives us. To make a request, email protosoftuk+privacy@gmail.com.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

To remove everything the app has stored on your phone, uninstall it, or use Android's Settings › Apps › SafeSpeed UK › Storage › Clear data.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
